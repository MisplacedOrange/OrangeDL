# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

## Overview

OrangeDL is a local desktop download manager. This project does not provide an OrangeDL-hosted user account system, subscription platform, or cloud storage service.

## Information the application may store locally

When a user runs OrangeDL, the application may store data on that user's device, including:

- Download URLs entered by the user
- File names and local save paths
- Download status, progress, speed, timestamps, and history
- App preferences and related local configuration

This information is used to operate the application and is stored locally on the user's machine.

## Network activity

OrangeDL may connect to third-party servers only when a user chooses to start, resume, retry, or otherwise manage a download from a URL they provide.

I do not control those third-party servers, their content, their logging practices, or their privacy policies.

## Data sharing

I do not sell personal data collected by OrangeDL.

Because OrangeDL is a local desktop application, any disclosure of downloaded content or saved metadata typically depends on the user's own device, operating system, storage setup, security posture, and chosen download sources.

## User responsibility

Users are responsible for:

- Choosing the websites, files, and services they access through OrangeDL
- Verifying the legality and legitimacy of the content they download
- Complying with applicable laws, contracts, copyright rules, and service terms
- Securing their own devices and local data

## No responsibility for illegal use

I do not control user behavior, the content users choose to download, or what users do with downloaded files after retrieval.

I am not responsible for piracy, copyright infringement, unlawful copying, unlawful distribution, or any other illegal or unauthorized activity carried out by users with or through OrangeDL.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project changes. The latest version in the repository will control going forward.
